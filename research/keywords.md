# Keyword clusters and URL map: RNC Landscaping

Research date: 2026-11-19. No search-volume tool was used, so **no volumes are given**. Priority (High / Medium / Low) is an estimate from population size, purchase intent, contract value, seasonality and the competition observed in `competitors.md`. Validate with Google Search Console data after launch.

## Method
- One cluster = one URL. No two URLs target the same cluster (several clusters may share a URL only when they are sub-intents of the same page, e.g. strata and public-sector buyers on `/commercial/`).
- **Service x area handling:** the contract has no combined service-area pages (e.g. no `/areas/saanich/irrigation/`). So each *service* page owns the regional service term ("irrigation victoria bc"), and each *area* page owns all "[service] + [area]" variants for that area ("tree removal saanich", "lawn care saanich"). Area pages must name all four services with a local sentence each (`servicesBlurb`) and link to the service pages; service pages link to all 13 area pages. This covers the 4 x 13 grid without cannibalisation.
- Neighbourhood terms ("gardener gordon head") live on their area page only.
- Resource slugs below are **proposed**; the copy agent owns final slugs. If they change, keep the cluster-to-page mapping one-to-one.
- Cost questions go to `/faq/` and are answered with how quotes are built, never with prices.

## Not mapped (deliberately)
- `/reviews/`, `/about/`, `/contact/`, legal pages: navigational/brand only; give them unique titles but do not target a cluster.
- `/portfolio/<service>/` and project pages: target long-tail project descriptions naturally; do not repeat service-page primary keywords in their titles.
- Snow removal, gutter cleaning, artificial turf, pressure washing: competitors offer them but they are outside RNC's four service lines.
