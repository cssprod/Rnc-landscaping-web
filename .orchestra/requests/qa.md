- **check-ci#required-gates is stricter (qa fix 1).** A gate counts only where the shell runs it as a command: not after `echo`, in a comment or inside quotes. A gate also fails the check when any of these holds:
  - the step or its job has an `if:` other than `success()`, `always()` or `!cancelled()`.
  - the step swallows the exit status (`|| true`, `|| :`, `|| echo`, `|| exit 0`, `exit 0`, `set +e`), or pipes the gate's output without `set -o pipefail`.
  - a `shell:` drops `-e`.
  - `continue-on-error` is set to an expression.
  - unit tests or Playwright run with a filter (`--grep`, `--test-name-pattern`, …).
  - Lighthouse runs with `--runs` below 3 or with `--allow-slow`.
  - a11y runs with `--pages` other than `all`, or without both themes and both motion states.
  Anyone who edits ci.yml must keep each gate as a plain command.

Prune with `BASELINE_LEDGER=<scratch dir>` and `--prune-baseline` on each build, then `node tools/lib.mjs --apply-ledger=<scratch dir>`. That keeps every entry that still fails in at least one build. A plain `--prune-baseline` on one build would drop the other build's entries. A cross-build entry is reported as stale on the build where it does not apply. That is expected.
