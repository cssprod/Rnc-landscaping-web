**Proposer:** P2 (W0 art direction lane). **Status:** proposal for J1/J2/J3 and S1. It changes no repo file except this one.
**Specimen and tooling** (scratch, reproducible): `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-Rnc-landscaping-web/5110250c-2871-5a49-ba20-c506ac0ea22b/scratchpad/direction/P2/`
- `palette.mjs` is the single source for both themes. `build.mjs` builds the specimen from it, and `contrast.mjs` checks it.
- `build.mjs` writes `specimen-dark.html` and `specimen-light.html` from one DOM. Only `data-theme` differs between them.
- `contrast.mjs` reports 198 pairs with 0 failures. `contrast.mjs --md` writes the table in the appendix.
- `tom.cjs` is the text-over-media check. It hides the text, screenshots the hero, and compares the hero copy with the worst real pixel behind it. It passes at 320, 390, 1024 and 1440 in both themes.
- `count.cjs` counts infinite animations in the viewport.
- `shoot.cjs` takes the 8 screenshots.

## 0. The idea in one paragraph
RNC's craft is reading land: slope, drainage, sun, soil and the local rules. The site should read as a **field survey made beautiful**: a living night landscape whose hills carry glowing contour lines, a glass instrument plate holding the **real service-area map** (the 13 `TILES` polygons from `site/src/components/map.mjs`, each shrunk toward its label point to form nested "hill" contours), spec sheets with hairline rules and tabular figures, numbered sections, registration crosses and a faint survey grid. "Web3" here means glow, glass, depth and living motion only. **Honesty rule baked into the aesthetic:** a survey sheet carries labels, grid references and legend keys, never live readouts. There are no coordinates, metres, percentages, live clocks or "scanning" UI. Every number on screen is true and comes from content: 4 services, 13 municipalities, 01–06 list indices and section numbers.

### 1.2 Two-tone glow focus (mandatory)
`:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--focus-ring); outline-offset: 2px; box-shadow: 0 0 0 4px var(--focus-halo), 0 0 18px 6px var(--focus-glow) }`
- **Dark:** the ring is camas-light `#CFC9FF` and the halo is camas-deep `#2E2296`.
- **Light:** the order swaps.
- Ring vs halo is **7.53:1**.
- On every content surface, and on the contour teal, white, the brand button and the scene sky and ridges, at least one tone is ≥ 3:1. The worst case is the light contour at 3.26.
- `forced-colors` falls back to `outline: 2px solid Highlight; box-shadow: none`.
- This fixes the blueprint finding that `#5A4FCF` on Fir night measures 2.83:1.

### 1.3 Contrast summary (WCAG 2 relative luminance, script-computed)
- **Dark:** minimum text 5.51, glass 5.39, focus 6.78, non-text 3.35.
- **Light:** minimum text 4.71, glass 5.19, focus 3.26, non-text 3.11.
- **Tightest pairs.** Use these only where the table shows they pass:
  - light `ink-3` and `success` on `bg-tint`: 4.71–4.72;
  - light `line-strong` on `bg-tint`: 3.11.
- **Glass worst cases** are checked as the material alpha composited over the brightest art pixel that can sit behind it (dark: the moon `#DDF5EA`, contour teal, a brand button scrolling under the header) or the darkest (light: the fore ridge, the deep CTA band under the header).
  - To pass, I raised the dark `material-regular` and `material-chrome` alphas to **.80** and the light `material-regular` to **.78**.
  - Every material has a solid fallback, used under `prefers-reduced-transparency` and `prefers-contrast: more`.
- **Living CTA band:** `on-deep-2` against the brightest blob peak with its fixed scrim is 7.96 (dark) and 7.52 (light).
- **Text over the living hero** is measured on real pixels by `tom.cjs`, which takes the worst single pixel inside each text box:
  | Theme | H1 | H1 italic accent | Lede | Eyebrow |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Dark | ≥ 8.1 | ≥ 5.9 | ≥ 4.93 (at 320 px) | ≥ 10 |
  | Light | ≥ 10.5 | ≥ 5.8 | ≥ 4.82 (at 1024 px) | ≥ 5.6 |

  Three changes were needed to get there. Ship them as rules, not tweaks:
  1. **Text-safe star mask.** Stars are masked to 10 % behind the copy column: vertically below 64em, horizontally above 64em. The ridge crest rim is masked the same way above 64em.
  2. **Aurora capped** at 55 % opacity below 64em, using the `--au-hi` keyframe variable.
  3. **Orb and moon-glint layers hidden below 64em**, where any sky position collides with the copy. Light-theme water and range were lifted too (`#A9C1C7`, `#BCC8D2`).

  qa's `text-over-media.mjs` should use the same worst-pixel method.

## 2. Type
- **Inter variable** (one file). `font-optical-sizing: auto`, so opsz 14–32 does the Display/Text switch by itself. Base features are `"cv11","ss03"` (single-storey a and round quotes).
- **Numbers that act as data:** labels, indices, the spec strip and map grid letters use `font-feature-settings:"tnum","zero","case"` with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums` (utility class `.tnum`).
- **Weights:**
  | Use | Weight | Tracking | Leading |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Body | 400 | -.006em | 1.6 |
  | Labels | 520–560 | +.01em | — |
  | UI and dd values | 600–620 | — | — |
  | H2/H3 | 660 | -.02 to -.028em | — |
  | Display | 680 | -.04em | 1.0 |

  This stays inside the requested 400–720 range. Sentence case only, with no all-caps labels. The "survey" voice comes from tabular figures, hairlines and small 13 px labels, not from capitals.
- **Fluid C3 scale** (`clamp`, 360 → 1440):
  | Step | Size | Value |
  |---|---|---|
  | display | 2.5 → 4 rem | `clamp(2.5rem,1.95rem + 2.3vw,4rem)` |
  | h1 (inner pages) | 2.0 → 3.25 rem | — |
  | h2 | 1.5 → 2.25 rem | — |
  | h3 | 1.25 → 1.5 rem | — |
  | body | 1.0625 → 1.125 rem | — |
  | small | .875 rem | — |
  | label | .8125 rem | — |

  The hero display is capped at **4 rem**, not the blueprint's 4.5. At 4.5 the 51-character home H1 ran to 5 lines and pushed the copy into the land (shown in the first specimen pass). Measures: 17ch for the H1, 42ch for the lede, 58–65ch for body.
- **Display treatment ("the surveyor's annotation").** The H1 ends with one **Newsreader italic 400** phrase at 1.06em in `color-brand`, for example "*in Greater Victoria*". It reads like a hand note on a technical sheet.
  - Rationed to **one phrase per page**: the H1 accent and the CTA band line.
  - Also used for client quotations, as now, and for map water labels ("Juan de Fuca Strait") at 13 px.
  - This needs one brand.md change, from "Newsreader italic for client quotations only" to "…quotations and one display annotation per page". The accent passes ≥ 5.8:1 on real pixels.

## 3. Layout grammar
- **Survey sheet frame:**
  - Content max 1200 px, gutter `clamp(1rem, …, 2.25rem)`, section rhythm `clamp(4rem, …, 8rem)`.
  - A **64 px survey grid** (1 px `survey-grid`, about 5 % alpha) is drawn only in the hero and in the card art wells, and radially masked so it fades out. It is never under body text.
- **Numbered section heads** (`.sno`): `02 · What we do ─────────── 4 services`.
  - The number is brand at weight 640 with tabular figures. The label is ink-2. A hairline runs to a 7 px end tick, and an optional true count sits on the right.
  - The H2 follows (max 24ch), then the lede (max 58ch).
  - Numbering is per page and sequential, with the hero as 01. On the screen it is decorative; the heading text carries the meaning.
- **Bento:**
  - 12 columns from 48em up, one column below. Cards are `container-type: inline-size` and reflow internally with `@container`; the scope list goes to 2 columns at ≥ 30 rem.
  - **Home recipe:** a 7×2-row feature card, then 5 + 5 stacked, then 5 + 7 (wide "How we work" card).
  - **Rules:** at most one tall feature card per bento. Every card has the same top row: an index like `S-01` (brand, tabular) and a short tag. Then an **art well** with grid background and a unique hairline motif, the title, the body, and a footer link above a hairline rule. The whole card is clickable through the link's `::after`.
  - **Service motifs** (each about 0.4–0.9 KB): mown stripes (maintenance), stepped terraces (installation), drip line with spray arcs (irrigation), tree rings (tree work). Area cards use that municipality's own tile contours.
- **Spec strip** (projects, case studies, the home featured project):
  - A ruler bar on top: a 16 px minor and 80 px major tick pattern in CSS gradients, with no markup.
  - A 300 px illustration thumb carrying the "Illustration" chip, then the badge ("Sample project" when `sample:true`), the title and the summary.
  - A `<dl>` in auto-fit columns (min 150 px) with hairline separators and a tabular index `01–04` in the top-right of each cell (`data-n`, decorative).
  - Fields come only from content: municipality, service, client type, duration and season. **No budgets, areas, durations in hours or invented metrics.**
- **Glass vs solid (C3-compatible):**
  - **Glass only on chrome and instruments:**
    - the header capsule;
    - the mobile sticky Call/Free-quote bar;
    - menus and the sheet;
    - the hero instrument plate, ≥ 64em only.
  - **Content cards are solid** `bg-elevated` with a 1 px `line` ring and an inset `glass-rim` highlight. They get depth from the glow on hover, not from translucency.
  - Below 64em there is never a `backdrop-filter` over the animated scene. The plate is hidden there; home's map section carries the map instead.
  - Mobile glass count is 2 (header and sticky bar), against a budget of ≤ 3.
- **Registration crosses** (`.reg`, 14 px, pure CSS) mark the corners of instrument plates only. Keep them rare.
- **Deep bands:** the CTA band and the footer use `deep-2` with a static grain tile at 5 % and a fixed left-to-right scrim. Never a blend mode.

## 5. Living landscape hero ("surveyed night / surveyed dawn")
One DOM, `aria-hidden`, with a visible "Illustration" chip. The H1 is the LCP element and is never animated. Each layer is an **outer** `<svg class="L">` box with the shared `viewBox 0 0 1600 900` and `preserveAspectRatio xMidYMax slice`. Only the outer boxes, or the parallax wrapper `div.par`, animate, and only by transform or opacity.

**Parallax (T4):** the `.par` wrappers translate −4 / −7 / −11 / −15 % across the first 100vh with `animation-timeline: scroll(root)`, inside `@supports`, only when motion is on.

**Night vs dawn from one DOM.** Every fill and stroke is `var(--scene-*)` or `var(--survey-contour)`, and SVG gradient stops use `stop-color="var(--…)"`. The theme toggle swaps about 20 custom properties:
- **Night (bioluminescent):** teal contour lines with a 3 px `drop-shadow` glow on the contour group (dark only, static), a teal crest rim, an emerald and camas aurora, yellow-green fireflies and stars at full opacity.
- **Dawn (survey at first light):** ink-teal hairline contours with no glow, a warm sun bloom, pale cream motes at 55 %, white mist and stars at opacity 0.

**Measured cost:**
- Scene markup is **13.9 KB raw / 3.8 KB gz**, under the 16 KB hero budget. Stars as zero-length path dots and fireflies as `<use>` saved about 7 KB.
- Motion CSS is about 0.75 KB gz.

## 6. Build notes for W2 owners (learned in the specimen)
1. **Do not put `stroke-dasharray:1` on the resting state** of `pathLength=1` paths. Chromium drops the strokes in some captures (fullPage, and likely print). Keep the dash only in the keyframes and in the hidden pre-reveal state, with `animation-fill-mode: backwards`. Apply `pathLength` to `<path>` only: polygons drawn with `pathLength` showed as 1 px dashes, so convert them with `M…L…Z`.
2. **`vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke` breaks `pathLength` dash maths.** Use it only on undrawn rims.
3. **Do not run two transform animations on one element.** Split them into the `.par` wrapper (parallax) and the inner layer (drift).
4. **Keyframes can read custom properties** (`50%{opacity:var(--au-hi)}`). Use this to cap ambient intensity per breakpoint or theme without duplicating keyframes.
5. **Text-safe zones are a rule, not art direction.** `living` must mask bright speckle layers (stars, glints, orb) behind the copy column, and qa's worst-pixel check must cover 320, 390, 1024 and 1440 in both themes.
6. **Reveals:**
   - Use `view()` inside `@supports` with a from-state of `translate: 0 24px; opacity: .35`. Never start at opacity 0, so full-page tools and old engines still show content.
   - Never put a reveal on the hero or the LCP element.
   - The specimen uses an IntersectionObserver class only because file:// screenshots are taken with a scroll-through.
