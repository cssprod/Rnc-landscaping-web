# P3: "Spatial Bento Glass"

Art direction proposal for the RNC "Fir night" redesign (W0). Spatial-computing feel: the page is a dark room with depth. The living landscape is a **window** floating in it, foreground firs **step out of the frame**, and glass panels hover at different depths. Bento tiles are frosted glass lit by the cursor. Big variable-weight display type does the talking.

Everything below has been built and screenshotted (see §8). The specimen source and the generators live in the scratch folder `direction/P3/`: `palette.mjs`, `contrast.mjs`, `scene.mjs`, `build.mjs`, `shoot.cjs`, `specimen-{dark,light}.html`.

**Honesty.** There are no stats, counters, tickers, HUD readouts or invented RNC facts. The only figures shown are content facts already in the repo: 13 municipalities, and the sample project's "3 weeks" (labelled Sample). Placeholders render as chips (`[PHONE]`, `[REPLY TIME]`). Every artwork carries an "Illustration" chip, and the review and project are labelled "Sample".

Design intent:
- **Dark "Fir night".** Near-black fir green (`#07110D`), luminous mint brand (`#8FE3B0`), and camas violet used only for the focus ring and as the last stop of the edge gradient.
- **Light "Dawn".** Sea fog stays (`#F3F6F1`) and Douglas fir stays (`#1D4A33`). The camas glow becomes a peach dawn glow, so the light theme feels like sunrise rather than an inverted night.
- **Gradient edge.** fern → tide → camas (→ sun only for the conic ring). It is the single "Web3" accent: used on the H1 accent phrase, the cursor-lit tile borders and the two rationed `@property` rings.

### Glass rules (contrast-safe by construction)
- **Real `backdrop-filter`** is used in only 3 places:
  - the header capsule (`material.chrome`);
  - the mobile sticky bar;
  - the hero "Next step" card at ≥ 64em (`material.regular`).

  The hero card is the only one over animated layers, so below 64em it uses solid `material.thick`. That gives at most 2 per mobile viewport, within the ≤ 3 budget.
- **Bento tiles, the spec strip, the eyebrow and secondary buttons use "faux glass".** This is `glass.tile`, a translucent fill plus a rim and hairline, with **no backdrop-filter**. What sits behind them is the static stage: pre-blurred radial glows plus a faint spatial grid. Blurring something that is already blurred buys nothing, so we skip the cost. Contrast is computed on the composite of tile over stage peak over bg, including the cursor spotlight on top (worst case).
- Under `prefers-reduced-transparency` or `prefers-contrast: more`, every material turns `color.bg-elevated`, and the stage glows, grid and grain are removed. Under `forced-colors`, gradient text falls back to CanvasText, gradient rings are removed and the outline goes to the system colour.
- Text over art is never set directly on the art. Chips use `material.media-strong` + `color.on-deep`, and the floating card uses `material.thick` + `ink`/`ink-2`. Both are checked against the brightest and darkest art colours of each theme (moon, fireflies, dawn sky, fir).

### Focus ring (mandatory, two-tone "sandwich")
`outline: 2px solid var(--color-focus); outline-offset: 2px; box-shadow: 0 0 0 6px var(--color-focus-halo), 0 0 26px 8px var(--glow-camas)`

This draws halo B on both sides of ring A (B 0–2 px, A 2–4 px, B 4–6 px) plus a soft camas glow. A/B contrast is 11.76:1 in dark and 7.48:1 in light. On every surface, including art extremes and the brand-filled button, at least one tone is ≥ 3:1. The CTA band (always deep) overrides it to `focus-on-deep` with a deep halo. Under `forced-colors` it becomes `outline: 2px solid CanvasText`.

### Contrast results
`node contrast.mjs` uses WCAG 2 relative luminance with alpha compositing. It checks **250 pairs with 0 failures**. Minimums:

| Theme | Kind | Lowest pair | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| dark | text | `ink-3` on tile + spotlight over the fern stage peak | 4.72 |
| light | text | `success` on `bg-tint` | 4.79 |
| dark | non-text | `line-strong` on `bg-elevated` | 3.64 |
| light | non-text | `line-strong` on `bg-tint` | 3.32 |
| dark | focus | best tone vs any surface | ≥ 9.99 |

## 2. Type
- **Inter Variable** (`opsz` 14–32, `font-optical-sizing:auto`, so the Display cut appears automatically at headline sizes) in the 340–720 range. **Newsreader Variable italic** is used only in review and quote blocks (C3).
- **Fluid C3 scale** (clamp, 360 → 1440):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| display (H1) | `clamp(2.5rem, 1.62rem + 3.1vw, 4.5rem)` | **690** | −0.042em | 1.0 |
| h1 (inner pages) | `clamp(2rem, 1.6rem + 1.9vw, 3.25rem)` | 680 | −0.036em | 1.04 |
| h2 | `clamp(1.75rem, 1.3rem + 1.7vw, 2.75rem)` | 680 | −0.035em | 1.04 |
| h3 | `clamp(1.25rem, 1.15rem + .4vw, 1.5rem)` | 650 | −0.022em | 1.15 |

| small / label | 0.875rem / 0.8125rem | 540–620 | 0 | 1.4 |

  The h2 is one step above C3's 2.25rem cap. This is deliberate for the spatial feel; ds-core may clamp it back to 2.25rem.
- **Display treatment: weight contrast inside one H1.**
  - The subject is set at 690. The place phrase drops to **340** and takes the fern → tide → camas gradient: "Landscaping, grounds care, irrigation and tree work *in Greater Victoria*".
  - The H1 is never animated (it is the LCP element). The gradient text has a solid fallback colour, and under forced colours it becomes CanvasText.
  - Gradient-stop contrast against `bg` is ≥ 11.3:1 in dark and ≥ 5.2:1 in light; each stop is checked at 4.5:1 even though it is display-size text.
- **Numbered section heads.** A glass pill with tabular figures ("01", "02", "03") plus a sentence-case label, then a hairline that fades out. These are labels, not counters.
- **Living type (Optional, T4).** Section H2 weight settles 380 → 680 on a `view()` timeline as the section enters. It needs an L1 measurement, because weight changes cost layout per frame. It is one heading per section and one shot; the static weight is 680.

## 3. Layout grammar
- **Containers.**
  - `wide` = min(1360px, 100% − 2·gutter) for the hero and CTA band.
  - `wrap` = 1200px for content.
  - Gutter: `clamp(1rem, .6rem + 1.8vw, 2.25rem)`.
- **Section rhythm.**
  - `padding-block: clamp(56px, 6vw, 104px)`.
  - Every section is a numbered head, then an H2 + intro on a two-column split ≥ 64em (H2 left, lede right-aligned), then the body.
- **Bento rules.**
  1. A 12-column grid with 16px gaps. Tiles span 7×2 (feature), 5, 5 on the first two rows and 4/4/4 on the third. Reflow uses **container queries**: 6 columns at ≤ 900px (the feature tile spans 6; the others span 3 or 6), and 1 column at ≤ 600px. It works at 320 px.
  2. Concentric radii: tile 26px, art well 18px with 8px inset (26 − 8), chips and pills capsule.
  3. Every tile has an **art well** (a theme-reactive mini SVG vignette), then H3, one sentence and a link. The link's `::after` makes the whole tile clickable, and the link text is the accessible name.
  4. At most one "odd" tile per grid: a Newsreader review (labelled Sample) or the area constellation (13 municipalities, positions from real lon/lat, `:has()` lights a dot when its chip is hovered or focused).
  5. Tiles sit on a **stage**: static pre-blurred glows plus a radial-masked 48px grid, the "spatial floor". The stage is never animated.
- **Spec strip.** A glass rail `<dl>` with 4 cells (Municipality / Service / Client / Duration, or Season once content has it). Each cell is a small diamond bullet, then the label (`ink-2`, 13px), then the value (title-ish, 620). The layout is 2×2 below 64em and 1×4 above, with hairline dividers. Values come only from project JSON, and the section is labelled "Sample project".
- **Glass vs solid.**
  - Glass is used where depth is the message: chrome, the floating card over the scene, bento and spec over the stage.
  - Solid is used for long reading (guides, FAQs, quote form, local-rules callout). Those use `bg-elevated` so contrast is predictable (C3).
  - Deep bands (CTA, footer) are solid `deep` with a fixed scrim.

## 5. Living landscape hero (one DOM, two worlds)
**Structure.** `.stage > .win` (clipped, rounded 34px, glass rim) holds 13 outer `<div class="ly"><svg>` boxes. Each SVG uses `viewBox 0 0 1600 1000` with `xMidYMax slice`. The boxes are 112% of the window height, so parallax never shows an edge. A sibling `.pop` holds the frame-breaking firs, and `.card-next` floats on top.

| # | Layer | Night (bioluminescent) | Dawn | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | sky + orb | 3-stop deep teal sky; small moon high | blue → rose → peach; the **same circle** becomes the rising sun: `transform: translateY(var(--sc-orb-y))` drops it behind the range, `--sc-orb-s` enlarges the glow | none (theme change animates the orb 0.6 s) |
| 2–3 | stars A/B | 19 + 19 points | `opacity: var(--sc-stars)` = 0 | T2 breathe 7 s / 9 s, out of phase |
| 4 | veil | aurora ribbons (fern + camas) | cloud strata (white + peach) | T2 drift 70 s alternate |
| 5 | range | the Olympics across the strait, moonlit snow, mint rim light | lavender range, white snow, warm rim | T4 parallax (depth .12) |
| 6 | water + Gulf Islands | ink water | pearl water | T4 .16 |
| 7 | glade | moon glade under the orb (mint) | sun glade (white) | T2 shimmer 8 s |
| 8 | headland + firs | silhouettes, faint fern rim | sage greens | T4 .26 |
| 9 | mist | faint mint haze | morning fog | T2 drift 48 s |
| 10 | near shore + **tide line** | glowing cyan surf: the bioluminescent beach | white foam line | T4 .42 |
| 11 | fore | near-black plateau and rocks | deep fir | T4 .6 |
| 12–13 | motes A/B | fireflies (lime core + glow) | dawn pollen/dew, 75% opacity | T2 blink 4.2 s / 5.6 s (≤ 0.5 Hz) + float |
| pop | 3 slender firs, tallest breaks the window top by ~45 px | moonlit rim via `drop-shadow(0 -1.5px 0 var(--pop-rim))` + fern halo, so it reads against the dark page | warm rim, dawn halo | T4 with the fore depth |

**Theme mechanics.** Every fill and stop is a class (`.f-range{fill:var(--sc-range)}`, `.s1{stop-color:var(--sc-sky-1)}`), so `[data-theme]` swaps the whole world with zero DOM change. The theme toggle's circular View Transition makes night → dawn a sunrise.

**Animation mechanics.** Three animation sources never fight because each uses its own property:
- the entrance uses `scale` (T1, 1.6 s dolly-in, skipped on the fore and pop layers so the frame-break stays aligned);
- scroll parallax uses `translate` (T4, `scroll(root)` over 0–100vh, `--d` depth);
- ambient loops use `transform`/`opacity` on the inner SVG (T2).

All of this is composited only. No `filter: blur`, no SMIL and no inner-node animation (only the outer boxes animate).

**Weight.**
- The scene is 15.7 KB raw plus 0.3 KB for the pop layer: 16.06 KB in total (4.5 KB gz).
- That is within the hero-scene budget of 16 KB raw, with about 300 bytes to spare.
- It is generated deterministically by a seeded RNG, with ids namespaced `p3*`.

**Accessibility.**
- The window is `role="img"` with an "Illustration: …" label, the "Illustration" chip is visible, and every layer is aria-hidden.
- The H1 is outside the art, so it stays the LCP element and is never animated.
- Without JS or with motion off, you get the static frame shown in the `*-static.png` shots.

## 6. Banned or avoided here
No tilt (the depth is parallax plus layering, not pointer tilt), counters, tickers, marquees, typewriter, glitch, custom cursor, HUD readouts, loaders, video, Lottie, WebGL or canvas, and no `filter: blur` on animated layers. No prices.

## 7. Risks for J2 / L1 to measure
- `@property --angle` rings repaint their element each frame. There are only 2, and both pause off-screen.
- The H2 weight settle (Optional) causes layout per frame. Drop it if L1 sees more than 2 ms.
- The hero has 13 composited layers. Memory cost is about the window area × 13 at DPR 3. If L1 flags it, merge stars A+B and motes A+B (11 layers, 6 loops).
- `drop-shadow` on `.pop` is rasterised once (the layer only translates), but L1 should confirm there is no re-raster during the parallax.

## 8. Specimen and screenshots
Each specimen is a self-contained HTML file per theme (identical DOM; only `data-theme` differs). It contains the header with the Dark theme and Animations toggles, the hero, the bento services, the featured project with its spec strip, the CTA band, a footer and the mobile sticky bar.

Measured on the specimen:
- **Weight:**
  - The page is 19.2 KB gz in total.
  - The inline CSS is 8.6 KB gz, of which about 1 KB is specimen-only tile art CSS.
  - The JS is 0.7 KB gz: toggles, the view-transition reveal, IntersectionObserver pause, hidden-tab pause and the spotlight.
- **Layout:** no horizontal overflow at 390 or 1440.
