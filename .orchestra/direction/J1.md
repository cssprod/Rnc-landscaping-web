# J1 judge: conversion and honesty

**Lens.** Does the direction get a visitor on a phone to request a free quote, through clear calls to action, credible trust signals, readable copy and fast comprehension? Does it avoid anything that could read as fake, inflated or crypto-coded for a local trades business?

**Evidence read.**
- PLAN.md, EXECUTION-SPEC.md, blueprint C3, brand.md (voice).
- P1.md, P2.md, P3.md.
- `.orchestra/reports/effects-lab.json`.
- Every specimen screenshot: P1 ×8, P2 ×8, P3 ×8, and P3 `dbg/` ×6. The full-page shots were cut into readable crops in `scratchpad/j1crops/`.

Specimen copy was cross-checked against `content/**`:
- `pages/quote.json`, `pages/home.json` and `pages/faq.json`;
- `services/grounds-maintenance.json`;
- `projects/*.json` and `testimonials/*.json`.

I also ran a Playwright check of overflow and CTA height on all 6 specimens at 320 and 390 (`scratchpad/j1-overflow.cjs`).

## Rubric (weights sum to 100)
| # | Criterion | Weight | What earns a 10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | First phone viewport (390×844) | 20 | What, where, primary CTA and Call all visible without scrolling. Art supports rather than delays. |
| B | CTA system | 20 | One clear primary action repeated at the right moments: header, hero, sticky bar, end band. Tap targets ≥ 48 px. A path for undecided visitors. No duplicate or competing CTAs. |
| C | Trust and proof (honest) | 20 | Answers "do you serve me?", "what happens next?", "how fast?" and "is there a catch?" using only content-backed facts. Samples and illustrations clearly labelled. |
| D | Readability and comprehension | 15 | Plain language and calm hierarchy. No jargon or numeric noise. Sequential structure. Reflows at 320. |
| E | Honesty and anti-hype | 25 | No fake telemetry, HUD or "data" look. No crypto-coded gloss. No claims beyond content. Brand voice kept (calm, precise, neighbourly). |

### P1: 8.0
**Strengths**
- At 390 the H1, lede, a 52 px "Get a free quote", Call and three trust ticks are all above the fold. The ticks ("Local rules, handled · A written scope · One contact") are lifted from `home.json`.
- The emotional, local world (night cove, moon path, firs, glowing surf) reads as *Salish Sea*, not *tech*. It is the least crypto-coded of the three.
- The CTA band copy is accurate: "We'll get back to you within [REPLY TIME] to arrange your free quote" matches `quote.json`.
- The "Not sure which?" bento card is a good route for undecided visitors.
- The Sample tag is mandatory in the spec strip, and the Illustration chip is on all art.
- Content cards are lit solid, so contrast is predictable.

**Weaknesses**
- The mobile header has no Free-quote button.
- Home never shows the 13 municipalities, so "do you serve me?" goes unanswered.
- There is no "no obligation" reassurance.
- The mobile bento order reads 01, 02, 04, "Not sure", 03, which is confusing.
- The comet ring on the hero CTA uses `@property`, which L1 marks *avoid*. Use the rotated-conic alternative.

### P2: 7.2
**Strengths**
- The best honesty statements:
  - the footer line "Illustrations are generated art, not photos of RNC projects";
  - "Schematic, not to scale" on the map;
  - an explicit no-readouts rule.
- The service cards carry the richest content-sourced copy: bylaw checks, CRD watering days, and the scope list from `grounds-maintenance.json`.

**Weaknesses**

Every number is true, but together they read as data or telemetry. This is the aesthetic the banned list exists to prevent, and it slows comprehension on a phone.
- The service-area map has **no municipality names**, so it cannot answer "do you serve me?".
- **The CTA band overpromises.** "We reply within [REPLY TIME] with a written scope and next steps" is wrong: per `quote.json` the reply asks questions and arranges a site visit, and the written quote comes later.
- Section numbering jumps from 03 to 05.

### P3: 8.1
**Strengths**

  - Free quote in the header at desktop, and the hero CTA;
  - a floating "Next step: free quote, we reply within [REPLY TIME]" card beside the art;
  - a sticky Call / Free-quote bar;
  - a CTA band with a trust micro-line: "Reply within [REPLY TIME] · No obligation · 13 municipalities, Sidney to Sooke".

**Weaknesses**
- **Copy overreach in the hero.**
  - "Free on-site quote(s)": content says "site visit if needed" or "from a site visit or your photos".
  - "No prices online, no pressure": "no pressure" is not content wording; content says "no obligation".
- **The H1 gradient (fern → tide → camas violet) at weight 340** is the most crypto/Web3-coded element in any proposal. A purple-teal gradient on thin type is "token launch", not "local landscaper".
- The "spatial computing" violet glows push the same way.
- **The "Next step" card looks clickable but is not a link.**
- On mobile, the sticky bar and the hero CTA both show in the first viewport. The duplicate CTAs cost about 70 px.
- Two `@property` rings are planned; L1 marks those *avoid*.

## Vetoes
None. Vetoes belong to J2 only.

The following are **J1 must-fix conditions** for any hybrid. S1 should carry them into DESIGN-DIRECTION and the briefs.
1. **No HUD grammar.** Drop grid letters and numbers, registration crosses, `S-0n` codes, numerals on list items or spec cells, and right-aligned counts on section rules. Section numbers (01–n, sequential, decorative) are allowed. True content counts are allowed in prose ("13 municipalities"), not as data badges.
2. **No crypto-coded gradient text.** The H1 accent is a single colour (brand) or the P1 Newsreader italic phrase. Do not use a teal → violet gradient on body or display copy. Camas violet stays reserved for the focus ring.
3. **Copy must match content.**
   - Use "free quote" and "a site visit if needed", never "on-site quote".
   - Use "no obligation", never "no pressure".
   - The reply promise is "we get back to you within [REPLY TIME] to ask any questions and arrange your free quote". It never says "with a written scope".
   - qa's `check-honesty.mjs` should flag "on-site quote", "no pressure" and "reply … with a written scope".
4. **Anything that looks like a button is a link or a button.** The "Next step" card must link to `/free-quote/`.
5. **Any map shown on home or areas names the municipalities.** Either labels on the art or the P3 chip list, which is also the keyboard path. Keep "Schematic, not to scale".
6. **Mobile first viewport shows one primary CTA, not two.** Use P1's rule: the sticky bar stays hidden while the hero CTAs are on screen, and is always shown with no JS.
7. **The header reflows at 320 px.** At narrow widths the toggles are icon-only 44×44 buttons with visually hidden names (P1/P3 pattern), not text pills.
8. **Sequential section numbers** with no gaps.
9. **Sample labelling.** "Sample project" and "Sample review" chips sit adjacent to the content they label. No stars, ratings, review counts or Review/AggregateRating schema.
10. **Honesty footer line in every footer**, reworded so it does not suggest AI imagery: "Illustrations are drawn art, not photos of RNC projects."
