## 2. Vetoes (each one breaks a hard constraint or a lab "avoid" verdict)

**V1. Every proposal: an ambient `@property` angle loop.**
- **Where:**
  - P1: hero-CTA comet ring, `spin` 6 s.
  - P2: "How we work" conic border, 12 s.
  - P3: `.card-next` and `.cta` rings, 14 s.
- **Why:** The lab measured an `@property --angle` loop as never composited (UnsupportedCSSProperty): **151 ms/s main thread and 180 paints/s for 2 elements, continuously**. Verdict: avoid.
- **P1 also breaks the motion policy.** Its rule `html.js[data-alive]:not([data-motion=off]) .ring::before{animation:spin…}` is not inside the reduced-motion gate. I measured **1 infinite animation running under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` with no stored choice**, while the Animations toggle reads "off". That violates T2.
- **Allowed replacement:** a transform-rotated oversized conic layer inside an `overflow:hidden` card. Lab: 3.6 ms/s, composited, safe. Limit it to 2 per page; it counts toward the 8-loop cap.
- **Rule for ds-core:** every T2 selector goes through ONE shared gate in `95-motion.css`. qa lints for any `@keyframes` that animates a `--*` property.

**V2. Every proposal: `backdrop-filter` over animated layers, at any width.**
- **Measured** (rect overlap between each blurred element and each running infinite-animation target):
  - P1: hero "Call" `.btn-glass` over `.sc-aurora` at 320, 390 and 1440.
  - P2: `.hdr__bar` and `.sbar` over 5 animated layers at 320 and 390, and `.plate` over 6 at 1440. **P2.md §3 says "below 64em there is never a backdrop-filter over the animated scene", and that is false.**
  - P3: `.card-next` over 7 animated layers at 1440.
- **Why:**
  - EXECUTION-SPEC forbids this below 64em.
  - The lab measured 4.2× compositor work and a drop from **55 to 42 fps at 1440**. Its verdict is "avoid… at any width", and the proposed motion-audit lint bans it.
- **Fix:**
  - While over the live scene, use pre-tinted opaque material: P1/P2 `material.thick`, or P3 `glass.tile` faux glass.
  - The header gets real blur only after scrolling past the hero.
  - The sticky bar stays hidden while the hero CTAs are visible (P1's pattern), otherwise it is solid.
  - Cap blur at 16 px. The lab measured 16; every proposal uses 24 px with saturate 1.6–1.8.

**V3. P1: hero scene geometry tied to text flow, so CLS breaks budget.**
- **Measured:** CLS **0.64 dark / 0.48 light** at 390 with 4× CPU throttle. It comes from one shift at about 330 ms: `svg.sc-stars-a` moves y 174→177 and its height goes 670→667. The impact area is the whole sky layer.
- **Cause:** the scene layers are sized against the hero copy, so a late font swap moves them. Most likely the Newsreader italic in the H1 is what swaps.
- **Why it's a veto:** the budget is CLS ≤ 0.05 and Lighthouse ≥ 95. At 0.64 the perf score fails.
- **Fix:**
  - Scene boxes are sized independently of the copy (fixed aspect, or absolute to a min-height hero).
  - Newsreader gets a `size-adjust`/`ascent-override` fallback and a preload.
  - CI measures CLS under throttle with the font swap delayed.

**V4. P3: scroll-linked `font-weight` 380→680 on the H2.**
- **Measured:** the `wght` animation (fontWeight) is running on `H2` on a view timeline.
- **Why:** it is a layout on every scroll frame and never composited. Line re-wraps move the content below, and that counts as CLS (scroll is not "recent input").
- **Ruling:** vetoed outright. The weight stays a static 680.

**V5. P3: Dark theme / Animations pressed state shown by colour only.**
- **Where:** the desktop switch pills are grey vs green with no knob or position change. On mobile only the icon tint changes.
- **Why:** this fails **1.4.1 Use of Color**, and the state indicator is not checked for 1.4.11.
- **Fix:** adopt P2's filled vs hollow dot, or a knob that moves position, at ≥ 3:1 against the pill.

**V6. P2: reflow fails at 320.**
- **Measured:** `scrollWidth` 356 at a 320 viewport in both themes. The `.tools` group (two 58 px dot+icon pill toggles plus the menu button) pushes the **menu button to x 312–356, off-screen**.
- **Why:** this fails WCAG 1.4.10.
- **Fix:** at ≤ 22.5em the toggles become 44 × 44 icon buttons that keep the dot cue, as P3 fits them at 320.

**V7. P3: `material.regular` values.**
- **Dark `rgba(12,24,19,.66)`, composited over the bright art the card can sit on.** ink-2 must reach 4.5; ink-3 must too.

  | Art behind the card | ink-2 | ink-3 |
  |---|---|---|
  | motes #C9FFA8 | **3.46** | 2.62 |
  | moon #E6EEFF | **3.51** | 2.66 |
  | tide #62F5C8 | **3.85** | 2.92 |
  | rim #7FE3C4 | **4.10** | 3.10 |

- **Light `.72` over fir #163926:** ink-3 is **3.55**.
- P3's table has no regular-over-art rows, so the gap was not visible there.
- **Fix:** take P2's dark .80 (ink-2 over moon 5.41) and light .78, or use `material.thick` (.84 → 6.75).
- This becomes moot for the hero card once V2 makes it thick, but the token values themselves are vetoed.

**V8. Every proposal: scroll-scrubbed `stroke-dashoffset` contour draws.**
- **Where:** P1's "Sample projects" contours (9 `draw` animations on a view timeline, measured); P2's process traverse (`view()`); P3's area-callout contour.
- **Why:** these are non-composited, and the lab measured +41 ms/s while scrolling.
- **Allowed:** only as a one-shot draw (≤ 1.6 s) triggered by an IntersectionObserver class, and never in the LCP window.

**V9. P1: the entrance "rise" animates independent `translate` on `<svg>` layer elements.**
- **Measured:** `riseS`/`rise` on `svg.sc-far`, `.sc-mid`, `.sc-near` and `.sc-fore`.
- **Why:** the lab found that `SVGTargetHasIndependentTransformProperty` means this is not composited, and it runs during load, inside the TBT/LCP window.
- **Fix:** animate `transform` on the `<svg>`, or `translate`/`scale` on an HTML wrapper as P3 does. Start it after first paint.

**V10. Every proposal: `view()`-timeline section reveals when the page also has scroll parallax.**
- **Why:** the lab measured parallax at +31 ms/s and view() reveals at +36, which exceeds the proposed +60 ms/s scroll budget ("reveals must use IO").
- **Fix:** IntersectionObserver one-shot class reveals (+11 ms/s). The from-state is never opacity 0 (P2's rule), and reveals never apply to the LCP element.

## 3. Must-fix items that are not vetoes (S1 and owners)

**Specimen and policy**
- **P3's specimen hard-codes `data-motion="on"` and ignores reduced motion** (11 loops run under reduce). The build must use the frozen HEAD_SCRIPT.
- qa test: reduce plus no stored choice ⇒ **0 infinite animations**. This catches both P1's V1 and P3's hard-coded attribute.

**Toggles**
- P2 and P3 toggle accessible names are doubled ("Dark theme Dark theme"). Use one source for the name.
- P1's toggles are 34 px wide at 320. The spec requires ≥ 44 × 44.

**Fonts and the LCP H1**
- Newsreader in the LCP H1 (P1 and P2) puts a second 36 KB font on the critical path and was the source of P1's CLS.
- Fix: preload it with a size-adjusted fallback, or render the accent phrase in Inter (P3's approach).

**Filters**
- Static `drop-shadow` on SVG children (P2 dark contours and tile rings; P3 tide, motes, pop) is acceptable only while static. It must never run during a draw-in or a transition. Prefer a baked, wider, low-opacity stroke (P2's own fallback).
- Map hover: the drop-shadow switches on instantly, with no filter transition. The current-area pulse is an opacity loop on an HTML glow box (lab: 3.4 ms/s vs 76 ms/s).

**Pointer and view transitions**
- Spotlight: use the transform glow-child version (lab +43 vs +60 ms/s). Set `--mx`/`--my` only on the hovered card, never on `:root`.
- Cross-document view transitions: ≤ 3 names and ≤ 250 ms. P1's full-viewport `view-transition-name: scene` counts as one of the three. P1's 520 ms theme reveal is fine as same-document T1, and it is instant under reduced motion.

**Text over the scene**
- In P1's light theme, ink-2 over the far range is **4.32**, which fails, so the rule "copy never overlaps land" is mandatory.
- qa's worst-pixel text-over-media check must also run at scroll offsets where the parallax moves land toward the copy.

**Thin margins and leftovers**
- These pass with no margin, so never use them for state: P1 light `line-strong` on `bg-tint` is 3.01; P2 light ink-3 and success on `bg-tint` are 4.72.
- P1's bento numbering runs 01, 02, 04, —, 03 in DOM order. Number in DOM order.
- P2's section heads skip 04.

## 5. Gates S1 should write into motion-policy.md and the qa briefs (each would have caught a finding above)
1. Reduce plus no stored choice ⇒ `document.getAnimations()` shows 0 infinite animations. Plus a static lint: no `@keyframes` animates a `--*` property, and no infinite animation sits outside the shared T2 gate. Catches V1 (P1) and P3's hard-coded attribute.
2. No element with `backdrop-filter` intersects the rect of any running infinite-animation target, at 390 and 1440 (`audit.cjs` method). Catches V2.
3. CLS ≤ 0.05 at 4× CPU throttle with the web fonts delayed 500 ms. Catches V3.
4. `scrollWidth == clientWidth` at 320 in both themes, with motion both on and off. Catches V6.
5. check-contrast covers **every** material × {ink, ink-2, ink-3} over the art-palette extremes of each theme. Catches V7.
6. No keyframes on `font-*`, `stroke-dashoffset` or `filter` tied to a scroll or view timeline. Catches V4 and V8.
7. Toggle pressed state passes a greyscale screenshot diff: pressed and unpressed must differ visibly in greyscale. Catches V5.
