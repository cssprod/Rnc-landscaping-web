# J3: "Stunning and ALIVE" art-director verdict

**Judge:** J3, the art-direction lens. The question: is this genuinely cutting-edge 2026 visual design, with depth, glow, motion, richness, polish, coherence and memorability, while still feeling like a premium local landscaping brand?

**Inputs:**
- PLAN.md and EXECUTION-SPEC.md;
- blueprint C3 and brand.md;
- P1.md, P2.md and P3.md;
- `.orchestra/reports/effects-lab.json`;
- all 24 proposer screenshots, plus P3's `dbg/` extras.

**Method:**
- Every full-page PNG was cropped into 1300 px bands (desktop) or tiled columns (mobile) and read at full resolution. The crops are in `$SCRATCH/j3crops/`.
- I ran one Playwright overflow check on every specimen at 390 px (`$SCRATCH/j3crops/ov.cjs`). `scrollWidth` was 390 in all of them, so P3's 396 px static PNG is a capture artefact, not page overflow.
- No repo file other than this one was edited.

**Top pick: P1 "Bioluminescent Fir" as the base**, with P2's contour/map language and P3's depth and bento interaction grafted on (§5).

### P1: 8.4
**Why it wins.** It is the only proposal whose first viewport is *a place*. The full-bleed night cove is cinematic and instantly local:
- aurora rays;
- a crescent moon with a glint path on the water;
- sea-glass surf glowing along the shore;
- fireflies in the firs.

The H1 sits on open sky with a Newsreader-italic "*Greater Victoria*" glowing in moss. That one phrase gives the brand an editorial voice the other two lack.

The dawn theme is the best light theme of the three: a peach and honey wash, lilac Olympics and a sun rising behind the range. Night and dawn are the same world, not an inversion. The CTA band and footer stay deep in both themes ("a window into the night"), which ties the themes together. 8 loops, 14.7 KB scene, 3 glass elements: on budget.

**What holds it back (must fix in the build):**
1. **The hard seam under the hero.** At 1440 the land box ends in a flat horizontal cut into the page background (around y ≈ 945). Fade it with a mist band or a fore-ridge silhouette into `--bg`, never a straight edge.
2. **The far and mid ranges are outline-only.** They read as a wireframe next to the richly filled foreground. Give them tonal fills, a moonlit rim gradient and atmospheric perspective (lighter and bluer as they recede).
3. **The mobile first viewport is mostly sky.** At 390 the land is a sliver below the CTAs. Raise the land box or reduce the copy padding so the scene takes at least 28–30 % of the 844 px viewport. The rule "text only on sky" stays.
4. **Uneven vignettes.**
   - "Not sure which?" has an empty art well.
   - The tree-work fir is a hollow outline.
   - The featured project art is a dark rectangle with a moon.
   - Every art well must carry a full mini-scene at the hero's level of craft (see the P3 grafts).
5. **Icon-only toggles.** In light the spark icon reads as a "sun", so it looks like a light-theme control. At ≥ 64em show visible labels "Dark theme" / "Animations" (as P2 and P3 do). Below that, keep icons plus accessible names and a clearer pressed state.
6. **Dead air.** There is a large empty band between the CTA band and the footer at 1440. Tighten it.

### P2: 7.6
**Strengths:**
- **The most ownable idea in the round.** The real 13-municipality `TILES` map, shrunk into nested contour "hills" on a glass instrument plate, turns honest data into ornament.
- **Glowing contour ridges** in the hero are the most "2026 Web3" texture any proposal produced: glow lines over depth.
- **The tightest system:**
  - numbered heads with true counts ("4 services");
  - the ruler-bar spec strip;
  - tabular figures;
  - the CTA band built from real tile contours.

**Weaknesses (my lens):**
- **It reads as a data product, not a landscaper.**
  - Grid letters A–I and 1–8, registration crosses and "S-01" codes drift toward the HUD look the brief bans.
  - The light theme is a clinical blueprint: white cards with thin line art.
  - Service motifs (wavy lines, step lines, rings) are abstract enough that "Grounds maintenance" could be a fintech chart.
- **The signature plate vanishes below 64em.** The mobile hero is a generic contour strip.
- **Much of the "life" is one-shot draw-in.** L1 measured dashoffset as non-composited (~118 ms/s while drawing), so the alive feeling after 2 s is thinner than P1's.
- **Defect:** section numbering jumps from 03 (Projects) to 05 (Free quote); there is no 04.
- **Defect:** the moon is a flat disc half-hidden behind the plate.

### P3: 7.3
**Strengths:**
- **The most explicitly spatial depth model:**
  - the window floating in a dark room;
  - a fir breaking the frame;
  - a floating "Next step" card;
  - a static "stage" of pre-blurred glows under the bento;
  - cursor-lit gradient tile borders.
- **The warmest bento art of the three.**
  - The featured-project cottage with lit windows, a stone path and camas-dotted shrubs is the single most "premium local landscaper" image in the round.
  - The drip-line irrigation, terraces with blooms and the constellation "Where we work" tile (`:has()` lights a dot) are delightful.

**Weaknesses:**
- **The hero execution undercuts the concept.**
  - The frame-breaking firs are flat black jagged spikes, the largest objects on the page. They block the aurora and moon and look heavier and cruder than the rest of the art. In light they are dark-green spikes over a pastel scene.
  - Putting the landscape in a box makes it less immersive than full-bleed. The left half of the hero is a flat dark page.
- **Mobile is the weakest first viewport of the three.** The window starts below the fold and the sticky bar sits over it.
- **The gradient-text H1 is crypto-adjacent.** "Greater Victoria" at weight 340 with a fern → tide → camas gradient is the one element in the round that reads Web3-*crypto* rather than Web3-*visual*.
- **Alignment drift.** The hero is on a 40 px "wide" container while sections sit at 120 px, so the H1 and the section heads don't share a left edge at 1440.
- **The light vignettes are generic.** They drift into flat "SaaS illustration kit" style.

## 3. Vetoes
J2 holds the only veto, so I record **none**. **Flags for J2 / S1** (L1-backed, affecting what may be grafted):

| Item | Proposal | L1 finding | Required form if kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| `@property --angle` conic rings | P1 (hero CTA comet), P2 ("How we work"), P3 (×2) | **avoid**: +151 ms/s, never composited | Use the **transform-rotated conic layer** (safe, 3.6 ms/s). At most 2 per page. |
| `backdrop-filter` glass over the animated hero | P2 instrument plate ≥ 64em, P3 "Next step" card ≥ 64em, P1 hero Call button | **avoid**: 4.2× compositor, 55 → 42 fps at 1440 | Graft both as **lit-solid** (P1's card gradient plus rim/sheen) or `material.thick` with no blur. Real blur stays on chrome only. |
| Contour draw-in volume | P2 (hero contours, plate rings, bento motifs on reveal) | **ration**: non-composited, 120 paints/s while drawing | One-shot only. At most 12 paths in view at once. Start after load + idle, never during LCP. |
| Map `drop-shadow` glow | P2, P3, P1 area hub | **ration**: +72 ms/s on hover sweeps | Glow only on the single active tile. Bake the ambient pulse as an opacity loop on a pre-drawn glow stroke, not by animating the filter. |
| H2 weight "settle" (380 → 680) | P3 (Optional) | Layout per frame | **Drop.** |
| Parallax `translate` plus a `transform` loop on one box | P3 (three properties on one layer) | `translate` on `<svg>` fails to composite | Split them across wrapper and inner, as P2's §6.3 build note says. |

**Honesty flag for J1.** P3's review tile shows an invented testimonial ("The deer walk past the new garden now…") labelled "Sample review". Keep the tile's *design* but fill it from real content or a `[REVIEW]` placeholder chip.

## 4. Defects to carry into the W2/W3 briefs (visual)
1. **P1:** hero seam; outline-only ranges; mobile scene share; empty or weak vignettes; toggle labels; dead air above the footer (all in §2).
2. **P2:** the missing section 04; the moon disc clipped by the plate; the light theme too clinical.

## 5. Graft list for S1's hybrid: "Bioluminescent Fir, surveyed"

### Base: P1 (keep as is, with the §2 fixes)
- **Full-bleed living night-cove hero**, one DOM for night and dawn:
  - aurora rays and wrapper drift;
  - two star layers;
  - crescent moon becoming the rising sun;
  - glint path;
  - bioluminescent surf (one path, 3× `<use>`);
  - mist;
  - firefly / dew-mote layers;
  - the "text only on sky" rule.
- **Dawn palette** (peach/honey/lilac) as the light theme.
- **Newsreader-italic accent phrase** with a moss glow: one per page (H1 plus the CTA band line). P1 and P2 arrived at this independently, and it is the strongest premium signal in the round. S1 must rule on the change to C3/brand.md; I support it.
- **Deep CTA band and footer** in both themes.
- **Lit-solid cards** (gradient fill plus rim, edge and sheen). Real glass only on the header, sticky bar, menus and sheets.
- **Theme "moonrise" View Transition** from the toggle.
- **`view-transition-name: scene`** so the sky persists across pages (within L1's ≤ 3 names and ≤ 250 ms).
- **Sticky bar hidden** while the hero CTAs are visible.

### From P2: the second layer of meaning
1. **Contour instrument plate from the real `TILES`** (nested inset contours, markers, "Schematic, not to scale", water labels in Newsreader):
   - the **hero of the area hub**;
   - the **home "Where we work" section**;
   - the **"You are here" plate** on each area page;
   - render it lit-solid, never with blur over animation.
   - **Drop** the A–I and 1–8 grid letters and the registration crosses, or keep crosses at plate corners only, on one plate per page.
2. **2–4 glowing contour strokes on P1's headland and near-shore ridges.**
   - Bake the glow as a wider, low-opacity second stroke (no `drop-shadow`).
   - One-shot draw-in after idle, then static.
   - This fuses "bioluminescent" and "surveyed".
3. **A CTA band built from real tile contour rings**, layered with P1's pre-blurred aurora blobs.
4. **Numbered section heads** with a hairline, end tick and true right-aligned counts ("4 services", "13 municipalities"). Sentence case, as P2 does, not P1's all-caps.
5. **Spec-strip ruler bar** (CSS-gradient ticks, no markup), tabular figures and hairline cells.
6. **Glowing line service motifs** (terraces, drip arcs, tree rings) as the *overlay* on service heroes and the TOC "measuring tape". Not as the whole card art.
7. **Engineering rules:**
   - text-safe star/glint masks behind the copy;
   - `--au-hi`-style keyframe variables to cap intensity per breakpoint;
   - P2 §6 build notes (pathLength and dasharray at rest, split wrappers);
   - worst-pixel `text-over-media`.
8. **The "Off the map" 404.**

### From P3: depth and touch
1. **Bento interaction:**
   - cursor-lit spotlight plus a pointer-following **fern → tide → camas gradient border** on tiles, using L1's **transform version**;
   - the 3 px lift;
   - T3 only (fine pointer, motion on).
2. **The static "stage" under bento grids:** pre-blurred radial glows plus a radial-masked faint grid. It is the "spatial floor", never animated and free per L1.
3. **Vignettes to adopt or redraw in P1's hand:**
   - the **cottage with lit windows, stone path and camas shrubs** (featured project, and the template for per-project art);
   - drip-line irrigation with droplets;
   - terraces with blooms;
   - the **constellation "Where we work" tile** with `:has()` chip ↔ dot lighting.
4. **The frame-break motif, scaled down.**
   - Use it only on the featured-project card or service heroes: one slim fir, drawn like P1's firs with a moonlit rim, crossing the card's top edge by 24–40 px.
   - Never in the home hero, and never as black spikes.
5. **A floating "Next step" card** (Free on-site quote · We reply within `[REPLY TIME]`) at the bottom-right of the home hero at ≥ 64em, over sky.
   - Lit-solid with no blur.
   - It provides a second depth plane without a window frame.
6. **Cross-document morphs** from bento tile → service hero and project card → case-study hero (`view-transition-name` per slug, at most 3 names, at most 250 ms).
7. **The sandwich focus ring** (halo / ring / halo plus camas glow). It has the highest ring/halo contrast (11.76 dark, 7.48 light), and the halo is restyled `focus-on-deep` on deep bands.
8. **A subtle `scale` dolly-in entrance** (1.04 → 1, ≤ 1.6 s) on land layers. It is a separate property from parallax; measure it in L1's harness.

### Do not graft
- **P3:** the windowed home hero; the giant black frame-breaking firs; the gradient-text H1 at weight 340; the H2 weight settle; the invented review text.
- **P2:**
  - survey grid letters in the hero;
  - "S-01" service codes;
  - a survey grid behind body content;
  - the light theme's bare line-art cards;
  - glass over the animated hero.
- **P1:** the `@property` comet ring. Rebuild it as a rotated conic layer if S1 wants one ambient accent, and spend it on the hero primary CTA *or* the CTA band, not both.

**Hybrid loop budget (home hero viewport).** Keep P1's 8 loops. The ridge contours are one-shot, and the CTA band (3 blob loops) sits in its own viewport. Bento spotlight is T3, which means it is pointer-only and not ambient.
